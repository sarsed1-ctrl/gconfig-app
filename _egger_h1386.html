Color and character

H1386 Brown Casella Oak blends authentic wood character with modern simplicity. Its elegant floret pattern and soft, varied color tones make it easy to pair with both warm and cool palettes. The ST40 Feelwood Oakgrain texture highlights this natural look with a synchronized matte-in-matte pore, giving the surface the realistic appearance of an oiled veneer. The rich brown tone brings depth and warmth, echoing the timeless appeal of walnut.
